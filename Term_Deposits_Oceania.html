DCS / FCS Deposit Taker Database

NZ (DCS) and AU (FCS) deposit takers from the official lists. Minimum rate 5%.

A country button and a status button can be combined, for example AU Only + Approved. Click a lit button again to turn it off, or click All to clear both.

Add / Update Company

Select a row to edit it. Add creates a new company. Update changes the selected company. Enter the best rate you found and its term. A rate under 5% is blacklisted automatically.
